UPGRADE TO THE UPDATED GANESH FESTIVAL APP

1. Stop the currently running app (press Ctrl+C in its PowerShell window).
2. Back up your existing database file if it exists:
   ganesh_festival_app\instance\ganesh.db
3. Extract this updated ZIP.
4. Copy the updated files inside the new ganesh_festival_app folder over your current ganesh_festival_app folder.
   Keep your existing instance\ganesh.db file. Do not delete it.
5. Start the app with start_windows.bat. This version uses the `python` command (not `py`).
6. On first launch, the app automatically adds the Paid/Pending status column to your existing database.

PAYMENT RULES
- Pay: saves the contributor and marks them Paid.
- Pay Later: saves the contributor and marks them Pending.
- Send WhatsApp Message: opens an editable message and WhatsApp; it does not save the form entry. Use Pay or Pay Later when you want to save the person.
- Only Paid amounts count as Total Chanda Received. Pending amounts are excluded until the status is changed to Paid in Edit Person Details.

EXPENSE SUMMARY
The WhatsApp expense summary includes the itemized expense table, Total Chanda Received (Paid entries only), Total Expenses, and Remaining Chanda Balance.

WHATSAPP
The app prepares click-to-chat messages. You must press Send inside WhatsApp. Fully automatic sending requires the official WhatsApp Business Platform API.
